# Grade

## Definition
A colour grade applied by the renderer to all shots of a reel alike (planned as a LUT plus exposure adjustments).

## Not to be confused with
- [Palette](/domain/palette.md): what a phase asks for when choosing shots; the grade is applied after.

## Rules
- One grade per reel, never per shot.
