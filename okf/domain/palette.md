# Palette

## Definition
Part of each phase of the [emotional arc](/domain/emotional-arc.md): its colours and light, for example "warm golden hour, soft contrast". Search uses the shots' measured brightness, warmth and motion to keep the chosen shots close to it.

## Not to be confused with
- [Grade](/domain/grade.md): the colour treatment applied to the whole reel at render time.

## Rules
- One palette per phase.

Specs: `openspec/specs/reel-planning/spec.md`, `openspec/specs/shot-search/spec.md`.
