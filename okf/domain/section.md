# Section

## Definition
The part of the song the reel uses. The owner gives its start and end times; Claude can suggest sections when asked but never chooses one. The start time is also where the Instagram audio must start ([start-time note](/domain/start-time-note.md)).

## Not to be confused with
- A phase: one part of the [emotional arc](/domain/emotional-arc.md) inside the section.
- A song section in music theory (verse, chorus): the owner's section can start anywhere.

## Rules
- Times are used exactly, never moved, not even to a beat.
- Shorter than 20 or longer than 30 seconds: allowed only after the owner confirms.
- Times are song time, see [/domain/beat-grid.md](/domain/beat-grid.md).
