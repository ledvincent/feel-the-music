# Reel

## Definition
The finished product: a vertical video posted to the Instagram page. It covers one [section](/domain/section.md) of one song, shows shots chosen for the song's feeling, and carries the lyrics. The music is added in Instagram at the time given in its [start-time note](/domain/start-time-note.md).

## Not to be confused with
- A clip: one shot exported to the library, not a finished video.
- The reel plan: the JSON that describes a reel before it is rendered ([/data/reel-plan.md](/data/reel-plan.md)).

## Rules
- 9:16, silent, one shared [grade](/domain/grade.md).
- No shot appears twice in the same reel.
- Shots follow the song's feeling, not its literal words.
