# Domain

* [Beat grid](beat-grid.md) - The song's tempo, beat times and downbeat times, with word timings, all in song time: the clock every cut is placed on.
* [Cheap pass](cheap-pass.md) - The first analysis every shot gets: a look embedding, zero-shot tags and measured stats, plus junk deletion.
* [Contact sheet](contact-sheet.md) - One image showing a frame from each top search result, labelled with shot id and rank, to check search quality by eye.
* [Emotional arc](emotional-arc.md) - The few phases a reel goes through, each with a feeling, a visual direction and a palette, written by Claude for the section.
* [Expensive pass](expensive-pass.md) - The second analysis, only for shots that survived the cheap pass: a vision-language model writes a caption and structured details.
* [Grade](grade.md) - The one colour treatment applied to every shot of a reel at render time, so shots from different sources look like one film.
* [Moment library](moment-library.md) - The searchable collection of analysed shots built from every video placed in the inbox, grown over time.
* [Palette](palette.md) - The colour and light a phase of the emotional arc asks for, used to pick shots that look right together.
* [Reel](reel.md) - A silent 9:16 video of 20 to 30 seconds, made of short shots that match one section of a song, with the lyrics styled on screen.
* [Section](section.md) - The 20 to 30 seconds of a song a reel covers, set by the owner with exact start and end times in song time.
* [Shot](shot.md) - One continuous take cut out of a source video, the unit the library stores, searches and puts in reels.
* [Start-time note](start-time-note.md) - The note written next to each reel giving the song, the artist and where the Instagram audio must start for the lyrics to line up.
