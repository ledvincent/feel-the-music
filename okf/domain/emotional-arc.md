# Emotional arc

## Definition
Claude's plan for how the reel should feel over the section. It has a few **phases**; each has a feeling (from the [emotion list](/decisions/006-fixed-emotion-list.md)), a visual direction and one [palette](/domain/palette.md). The arc also names the **meaningful word** of each lyric line: the word that carries the line's feeling.

## Not to be confused with
- A beat: a pulse of the music ([/domain/beat-grid.md](/domain/beat-grid.md)). Phases used to be called beats in early drafts.

## Rules
- Phases follow the song's feeling, not its literal words: a line naming an object does not require that object on screen.
- Phases change on downbeats.
- Calm phases cut slower than intense ones.
